# Craft Interiors Showcase

Build a modern, innovative showcase website for "Craft Interiors & Wood Works", an interior design and custom furniture company. No shopping cart, no prices — this is a portfolio site to attract enquiries.

Style: clean, premium, minimal — neutral palette (beige/white/charcoal) with one accent color, bold imagery, smooth hover effects.

Pages:

1. Home — hero banner with tagline, category showcase grid, short "why choose us" section, testimonials placeholder, call-to-action to get a quote

2. Categories — Living Room, Bedroom, Dining, Lighting, Decor, Custom Wood Works — each showing 4-6 sample furniture photos (placeholder images), no prices

3. About Us — company story, craftsmanship focus, years of experience

4. Get a Quote — simple form: name, email, phone, upload photo/reference design, dimensions/size, message. On submit, opens email client (mailto) addressed to us with the details, or shows a "we'll get back within 24-48 hrs" confirmation

5. Contact — address, phone, map

Include a short inspirational section on Home: 2-3 lines about how good interior design transforms a house into a home, encouraging visitors to reach out for a custom consultation.
